Spend and approvals

Every request carries its site, its budget line and its full signature history. Returned requests carry the reason back to the raiser, never a silent rejection.

Where a site's chain includes a Procurement Officer, a line item with fewer than two vendor quotes blocks the whole requisition from advancing.

Approved spend commits against its site before money moves. Approving past a ceiling is allowed, but it notifies the Managing Director automatically.

Opens the moment the CFO releases payment. A milestone needs photographic or documentary evidence, and a rejected one reopens the original spend.

People

Nightly accrual, live balances and a two stage approval that stops at the Executive Director. HR sees every application read only, by policy.

Onboarding to exit with documents, confirmation, disciplinary record and deactivation. Account status stays separate from employment status.

Self assessment, supervisor scoring and a Managing Director verdict on one form, covering confirmation, promotion and salary increment.

Daily presence against the published holiday calendar. Leave is resolved first, so a person on approved absence is never marked absent.

Publish a role, collect applications on a public portal, shortlist and interview in one place. Everything after the application stays inside your permissions.

Generated from the approved role and grade, issued for signature and tracked to acceptance. An accepted offer creates the employee record automatically.

HR notices, policy nuggets and company announcements by email, push and in app sticker, with delivery tracked per person.

Revenue and CRM

Capture, qualify and convert with duplicate detection on the full international phone number. Leads from paid social arrive by signed webhook and route themselves.

The queue serves one lead at a time and the script branches on what the person actually said. No call can end without a dated next step.

Stages carry their own odds and every move is kept, so stuck deals and slow stages are visible. A deal lost always records why.

Locations, property types and budgets captured during calls, ranked against what you have available. Demand you cannot meet is counted too.

Signed money with real instalment dates, kept separate from weighted forecast. Leadership reads both and the two are never added together.

Register an event, raise its marketing spend, then file the report that justifies it. No further budget is released while a report is outstanding.

A won lead becomes a commission request that walks its own approval chain to the CFO for payment. Rates are configuration, changed once and applied everywhere.

What each client bought, what they have paid and what is still outstanding, with handover and title documents on the same record.

Operations

Daily and weekly reports route to the supervisor and escalate when late. Weekly rolls into monthly, quarterly and yearly without re entering a line.

Heads of department declare projections, blockers and the support they need. An unresolved blocker carries forward into next month automatically.

Assignment, acknowledgement, maintenance history and closed user group control. An unacknowledged device stays flagged until the holder signs for it.

Book a vehicle and driver against a trip. Mileage and fuel close out on return, so a trip cannot be quietly left open.

Any format uploaded becomes one uniform deck, presented in a synchronised boardroom where every screen follows the chair.

Governance

Guided calling

The queue decides who is called next, not the caller

A caller opens the queue and gets one lead. No browsing, no cherry picking. Skipping one takes a reason, and the reason is recorded.

  • Promises come first. Then today's due calls, then leads never rung, newest first.
  • Five attempts, at five different times. Five calls all made at ten in the morning miss the same person five times.
  • It is their list, not the company's. A telemarketer never lands on a lead another consultant has in negotiation.

The script

Your best caller's conversation, available to everybody

The script is a branching graph, not a laminated card. The caller taps what the person actually said and the system picks what comes next.

  • No call ends in nothing. Only do not call and wrong number close a lead for good.
  • Parked is not pending. Someone wanting a house you do not sell is parked against the day you have it, and counted as demand.
  • Edited without a developer. Change the pitch once and every caller has it on the next call.
  • The next step becomes real work. It lands on somebody's task list, not in a note nobody reads.

The case

Why this compounds

The market

Firms of 50 to 1,000 staff are numerous and structurally underserved. Global vendors scope for a dedicated IT function these companies do not have.

Why now

Regulatory pressure is pushing mid market firms toward auditable records, and the infrastructure barrier has gone.

The moat

The routing engine. Competitors ship one fixed path. Every company onboarded deepens the pattern library, so each deployment is faster than the last.

The model

Setup fees fund acquisition, annual licences compound, and premium modules expand revenue inside an existing account.

Use of funds

Multi tenancy and self service onboarding to remove the services bottleneck, a commercial team for Lagos and Abuja, and the controls that clear procurement review at larger accounts.

What exists today

A deployed system with audit logging, push, email and a configurable routing engine already in production. Not a prototype seeking validation.

Why it defends itself

Approval infrastructure is hard to rip out

Once a company's approval chains, budgets and audit history live in one system, replacing it means re documenting how the business makes decisions.

  • It sits across departments. Finance, HR, sales and operations all depend on the same records, so no single department can remove it.
  • The audit trail accumulates. Its value grows with age, and it cannot be migrated to a competitor without losing the history.
  • Expansion is natural. Companies start with requisitions, then add HR, then revenue, without a new procurement cycle.

Questions we expect

Is this a product or a services business today?

Today revenue mixes a one time deployment engagement with an annual licence. The use of funds is explicitly aimed at shifting that mix toward licence revenue by building multi tenancy and self service onboarding, which is what removes the services bottleneck on growth.

What stops a global vendor moving down market?

Price and process. Serving a 200 person Nigerian group profitably requires a deployment model measured in weeks and priced in naira. That is a different business from an enterprise ERP sale, and incumbents have repeatedly chosen not to build it.

How defensible is a workflow product technically?

The code is not the moat. The accumulated library of real approval patterns is, alongside the switching cost of a live audit history that spans every department in a company.

What is the concentration risk?

Real and acknowledged. The product is proven inside one multi arm group, which is a strong reference but a single one. Broadening the customer base is the primary objective of this round.
