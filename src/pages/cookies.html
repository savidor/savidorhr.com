Cookies

This site sets no cookies at all unless you accept analytics. There is no advertising, no retargeting, no social pixel and nothing that follows you to another site.

Last updated 25 September 2026.

If you decline, or ignore the banner

Nothing is set and nothing is loaded. The Google Analytics script is not merely blocked from storing anything, it is never requested, so no third party learns that you visited. The only thing kept is a single record of your choice, stored in your own browser so you are not asked again. That never leaves your device.

If you accept

Google Analytics is loaded and sets these:

CookieSet byPurposeExpires
_gaGoogle Analytics Tells one browser apart from another so visits can be counted2 years
_ga_<id>Google Analytics Keeps the state of the current visit2 years

These tell us which pages are read and roughly which country a visit came from. They do not tell us who you are, and we do not try to find out.

Changing your mind

Use the button below. It clears the choice stored in this browser and asks again on the next page you open. Declining also stops the analytics script loading from that point.

Cookies we do not set

No advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking, no embedded video or social widget that sets its own. The fonts and every photograph are served from this site rather than from Google Fonts or a stock library, so they set nothing either. The links to LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp are ordinary links: nothing is requested from those companies unless you click one.

The contact form

The form does not need a cookie and does not set one. What it does with what you type is covered in the privacy policy.
