How it works

Your approval chain, stored as settings

Most workflow tools hard code one approval path and make every company bend to it. SavidorHR keeps the chain as data, so you change who signs for what without anyone touching the software.

STEP 1

Someone raises a request

They fill in what they need and what it costs. They never choose an approver, because that is not theirs to decide.

STEP 2

The chain resolves itself

Role, department, site, category and amount decide the signature path, and the first approver is notified immediately.

STEP 3

Each stage signs or returns

A return carries its reason back to the raiser. Silence escalates, so nothing sits waiting on somebody's inbox.

STEP 4

Everything is written down

Payment closes the request and opens delivery tracking. The whole path lands in an immutable audit trail.

The routing engine

Different rules per site, without a developer

A property under a joint venture may need the Managing Director on every request. A workshop may need two vendor quotes over a quarter million naira. Both are rows in a table your IT manager edits.

  • First approver overrides. Replace the raiser's own supervisor for a specific site or category.
  • Ordered approval flow. Insert or remove a stage and every future request follows the new path.
  • Visibility rules. Control which departments can even see a site in the request form.
  • Price hiding. Decide whether staff price their own items or procurement sources them.

Built in

The parts nobody remembers to ask for

These are the things that decide whether a rollout survives its first quarter. They ship as standard, not as a paid add on.

Nineteen real roles

From Team Member to Managing Director, with Office of the MD, CFO, Executive Director, HR, IT and Procurement as first class roles rather than permission checkboxes bolted on afterwards.

Email and push, not just a badge

Approvers are notified on their phone the moment something needs them, and overdue stages escalate on their own to the level above.

Views are audited too

Most systems log changes. SavidorHR also records who opened a record, which is usually the question that actually gets asked.

Works on the phone in the field

Installable as an app, usable on a site visit, and every screen is built to be approved from a phone rather than only from a desk.

Break glass, safely

Emergency administration sits behind a PIN, is rate limited, snapshots any table before a destructive operation, and logs itself.

Migration from what you have

Staff lists, leave balances, open requests and client records are brought across during setup, so day one is not a blank system.
