Questions we get asked first

Why is there no price on this page?

A headcount of 60 and a headcount of 600 are different products, and four modules is not what twenty four is. One published number would be wrong for almost everybody reading it. Tell us the two facts and you get a real figure.

Can we start small and add modules later?

Yes, and most do. Modules switch on without a migration because they already share one permission model. You are quoted the difference, not a new licence.

How is a group with several arms priced?

Once, across all the arms in scope. You are not quoted separately per company, and staff who work across arms are counted once.

What does the setup fee actually cover?

Mapping your approval chains into the routing engine, importing your people and their reporting lines, and training the administrators.

What happens to our data if we leave?

Full export on request, in a format you can read without us. The break glass console also snapshots any table before a destructive operation.
