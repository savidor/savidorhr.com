  • Budgets check themselves. Approved spend commits against the site, and a ceiling breach notifies the MD.

Guided calling

No random calls, and no lead quietly written off

A pipeline is worth what somebody rings. The CRM hands out one lead at a time and will not let a call end without a date on the next step.

  • The queue chooses, not the caller. Promised callbacks first, then today's, then leads never rung.
  • Every outcome sets a date. Only do not call and wrong number end a lead for good.
  • The calls double as market research, including the demand you currently cannot meet.
