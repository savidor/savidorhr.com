Spend and approvals

Requests route themselves to the right signature

The person raising a request never picks their approver. The chain resolves from their role, their department, the site, the category and the amount.

  • Thresholds extend the chain. Under the limit a supervisor closes it. Over it, the same request continues to the ED, the CFO, then the MD.
  • Procurement inserts itself. Add a Procurement Officer to a site and its requests need two vendor quotes per line item before they can move.
  • Budgets check themselves. Approved spend commits against the site before money moves, and going past a ceiling notifies the MD.

People

One employee record, from offer letter to exit

Guided calling

No random calls, and no lead quietly written off

A pipeline is only worth what somebody rings. The CRM hands each caller one lead at a time, walks them through the conversation, and will not let the call end without a date on the next step.

  • The queue chooses, not the caller. Promised callbacks first, then today's, then leads never rung at all. Skipping one takes a reason.
  • Every outcome sets a date. Five attempts spread across different times of day, then a nurture date. Only do not call and wrong number end a lead for good.
  • The calls double as market research. Where people want to buy and at what budget, captured as you go, including the demand you currently cannot meet.
