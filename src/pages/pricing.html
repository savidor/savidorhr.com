Common questions

What does the setup fee actually cover?

Mapping your real approval chains with the people who sign them, migrating staff lists, leave balances, open requests and client records, configuring roles and routing rules, and training by role. It ends when you are live, not when a contract is signed.

How long does deployment take?

Three weeks is typical for Core and Operations. Enterprise depends on how many arms of the group are coming on at once and how clean the existing records are. We run in parallel with your current process before switching over.

What happens if we go over our headcount band?

Nothing breaks and nobody is locked out. We reconcile at renewal and you move to the next band from that date. We do not charge retroactively or per seat mid year.

Can we start with Core and add modules later?

Yes, and most companies do. Premium modules can be switched on at any point in the year and are billed pro rata to your renewal date, so your renewal stays a single annual event.

Where is our data hosted, and can we export it?

Hosting location is agreed at setup and can be in country where a policy requires it. You can request a full export of your data at any time, in a standard format, without a support ticket or a fee.

Do you charge for support?

Email support is included in every tier. Enterprise adds a dedicated line with a named contact. Configuration changes, such as changing an approval chain, are something your own IT manager does without involving us.
