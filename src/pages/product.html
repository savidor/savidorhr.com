The routing engine

Different rules per site, without a developer

One property needs the MD on everything. A workshop needs two quotes over a quarter million naira. Both are rows in a table your IT manager edits.

  • First approver overrides. Replace the raiser's supervisor for one site or category.
  • Ordered approval flow. Insert a stage and every future request follows it.
  • Visibility rules. Control which departments can even see a site.
  • Price hiding. Staff price their own items, or procurement sources them.
